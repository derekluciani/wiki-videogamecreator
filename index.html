Video Game Creator

A source-faithful synthesis of practical and conceptual knowledge for creative, commercially minded solo game creators.

I. Production and Sustainability

  • Project Scope, Risk, and Sustainable Development — How project duration, financial exposure, creative ambition, practical planning, and definitions of success shape sustainable game development.
  • Learning Game Development Through Constraints and Experiments — How constrained tools, small toys, analog prototypes, repeated attempts, and habitual exercises develop practical game-making and creative skills.
  • Code Organization, Performance, and Technical Constraints — How maintainability, runtime limits, batching, and technical illusion shape what a game can ship and display.

II. Game Design

  • Game Mechanics, Variants, and Transformations — How atomic analysis and changes to rules, dimensions, controls, topologies, simulations, and metaphors generate game variants and new rule structures.
  • Finding, Testing, and Refining Game Ideas — How game literacy, varied inputs, recombined influences, practical constraints, creative habits, and public prototypes help developers generate, select, and refine ideas.
  • Focused Game Design, Progression, and Player Agency — How a strong core, competing goals, theme–mechanic alignment, progression structure, playtesting, and reward cadence shape focus and player agency.

III. Markets and Positioning

  • Emerging Game Genres and Market Timing — How active demand, undefined genre boundaries, production cost, maturation, and discovery channels create temporary market openings.
  • Game Visibility, Steam Launches, and Creator Outreach — How public prototypes, creator-facing media, capsules, outreach, festivals, and release timing contribute to discovery.
