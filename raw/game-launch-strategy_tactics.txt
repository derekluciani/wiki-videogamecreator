Article title: Game Launch Strategy/Tactics
Author/Speaker: Payne Robinson
Date: 2026/01
Source Url: https://youtu.be/aY15f9oZxpE?si=VTmCWT8RxLlKurD5


1. Define success metrics: Establish clear goals, such as target sales or review counts, to set realistic expectations for your project.

2. Create a demo: Develop a playable prototype, alpha, or beta version of your game to gauge player interest.

3. Use an accessible platform: Host your demo on itch.io, which is favored for its low-friction browser accessibility and algorithm-driven visibility.

4. Drive traffic: Share your demo link across relevant communities on platforms like Reddit and YouTube to build a seed audience.

5. Monitor backend data: Regularly analyze your stats, specifically views, plays, and click-through rates. High traffic with low engagement often indicates that your thumbnail or title needs improvement, while low overall interest might suggest the game loop or onboarding needs refinement.

6. Iterate or pivot: Based on the data, decide whether to scrap the project, refine the build, or continue development if the metrics are promising.

7. Launch on Steam: Once you have a validated and polished demo, move your game to Steam. You can use the demo to gather wishlists and prepare for events like NextFest before the official release.
